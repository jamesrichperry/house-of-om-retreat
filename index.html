Daily Rituals

Your daily practice toolkit

Eight mindfulness and yoga practices to anchor your day, however simple, however brief.

01
Morning Meditation

Begin each day with 10–20 minutes of stillness before the world asks anything of you. Sit, breathe, and observe. Nothing else is required.

Daily
02
Pranayama Breathing

Nadi Shodhana (alternate nostril) and Kapalbhati to balance your nervous system. Five minutes of conscious breath changes everything.

Morning
03
Sun Salutation Flow

3–6 rounds of Surya Namaskar every morning. A complete practice in itself: warmth, breath, strength, and devotion in twelve postures.

Morning
04
Gratitude Journaling

Three things you are genuinely grateful for, written each morning. This small ritual rewires the mind toward abundance over time.

Morning
05
Yin Yoga Practice

30–60 minutes of long-held, floor-based postures to release deep connective tissue, calm the mind, and restore the body from within.

Evening
06
Walking Meditation

Take one walk each day without your phone. Feel each footfall. Breathe the air. Let the practice live in motion, not only on the mat.

Anytime
07
Yoga Nidra

The yogic sleep. 30 minutes equals 4 hours of rest. A guided body scan and visualization practice for deep restoration and integration.

Rest
08
Evening Reflection

A five-minute body scan before bed. Where did you feel open today? Where did you contract? Awareness at rest is the whole practice.

Evening
Going Deeper

Sustaining the inner work

The retreat was the spark. These are the ways to tend the flame. Beyond the asana, the real practice begins when life gets uncomfortable.

  • Commit to a fixed time for practice. Even 15 minutes changes the day
  • Create a small dedicated space in your home. A mat, a candle, that is enough
  • Join a local yoga community or studio for accountability and connection
  • Return to the Yamas and Niyamas when life feels unclear or heavy
  • Reach out to fellow House of Om alumni. Your sangha travels with you

The practice is not something you do.
It is something you become.
